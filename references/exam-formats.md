# Exam formats

This file is the formats half of the IELTS format pack. The pack contract and the index of packs live in [Format packs](format-packs.md), and other exams are added as new packs without changing the session engine.

## IELTS Listening

Four sections, 40 questions, about 30 minutes of audio. Section 1 is a conversation in an everyday social context, Section 2 a monologue on an everyday topic, Section 3 a conversation in an educational context, and Section 4 an academic monologue. Accents are mainly British, Australian and American.

Question types in rotation: form completion, note completion, table completion, multiple choice, matching, map or plan labelling, sentence completion and short answer.

A single section practice slice is 10 questions and roughly 7 to 8 minutes of audio, which is about 1,000 to 1,200 spoken words at a natural pace.

## IELTS Reading

Academic: three passages, 2,150 to 2,750 words in total, 40 questions, 60 minutes. Passages come from books, journals and magazines and grow harder across the test.

General Training: texts from notices, advertisements, handbooks and magazines, in three sections of increasing length, 40 questions, 60 minutes.

Question types in rotation: multiple choice, True False Not Given, Yes No Not Given, matching headings, matching information, matching features, sentence completion, summary completion, note or table completion, diagram labelling and short answer.

A single passage practice slice is 700 to 900 words with 13 or 14 questions and a 20 minute limit.

## IELTS Speaking

Eleven to fourteen minutes in three parts. Part 1 is an introduction and interview on familiar topics, four to five minutes. Part 2 gives a cue card with one minute of preparation and a talk of one to two minutes, followed by one or two rounding off questions. Part 3 is a discussion of abstract ideas linked to the Part 2 topic, four to five minutes.

In chat the parts map to staged prompts and voice note answers. Part 2 preparation and speaking times are enforced by the stage timer like any other section.

## IELTS Writing

Task 1, at least 150 words, suggested 20 minutes. Academic asks for a description of data, a process or a map. General Training asks for a letter.

Task 2, at least 250 words, suggested 40 minutes. An essay responding to a point of view, argument or problem. Task 2 carries more weight than Task 1 in the writing score.

## Session presets

- Quick drill: a single question with material scaled to it, such as a 150 to 300 word passage or a 30 to 60 second listening script, with or without a timer, scored as a raw result only.
- Single section: one slice as above, for daily practice of 10 to 25 minutes.
- Half mock: one listening slice, one reading passage and one speaking part, about 45 minutes.
- Full mock: all four skills at full length, about 2 hours 45 minutes plus scoring breaks, run only when the user asks for it.

## Adding another exam

TOEFL, EIKEN and similar exams plug in as another pack, following the steps in [Format packs](format-packs.md). The stage engine, the generation validation and the learner profile do not change.
